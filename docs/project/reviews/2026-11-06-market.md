# Kitokey — market and UX on six subjects, 2026-11-06

Every page below was read **2026-11-06** unless the row says otherwise. Nothing was installed or
run. It answers the decider's feedback of 2026-11-06 on six subjects, and builds on the reviews of
[2026-10-26 (market)](2026-10-26-market.md), [2026-10-26 (UX)](2026-10-26-ux.md),
[2026-10-30](2026-10-30-market.md) and [2026-11-03](2026-11-03-market.md); it repeats them only
where a subject needs it.

**Markers.** *Vendor*: a company about itself. *Rival*: a company about a competitor. *Secondary*:
press, a blog, a forum, a mirror. *Search only*: seen in a search engine's summary, the page not
opened. **F** a fact a page states · **I** the analyst's inference · **U** uncertain or not verified.
**Kitokey today** is read from the repository (`docs/adr`, `modules/shell`), not from a run.

Sizes: **S** under two days · **M** one deliverable · **L** more.

**F** No product read **plays a sound and checks that the system-audio capture heard it** (a
loopback check). Zoom and Meet test the output by ear; OBS shows a meter; Granola tells you after.

### What "audio not captured" costs

- **F** Granola documents the failure itself: missing grey bubbles = the call not heard; on
  Windows, "sound from other people on the call may not be picked up" with apps it does not
  support yet; in a virtual desktop only the mic ([Granola](https://docs.granola.ai/help-center/troubleshooting/transcription-issues) *vendor*).
- *Rival* (Anarlog, 2026-10-11): users "watched the window sit there, and found out after the call
  that nothing had been captured"; "appeared to be recording and were not, with no error and no
  alert"; "If the session never started, the meeting is gone" — because Granola keeps no audio
  ([anarlog.so](https://anarlog.so/blog/granola-ai-complaints/) *rival, quotes not traced to their origin*).
- **F** Wispr's notetaker: when the mic fails to resume, "only system audio is captured" for that
  stretch ([Wispr docs](https://docs.wisprflow.ai/articles/3089221553-troubleshooting-notetaker-recording-and-audio-beta) *vendor, search only*).
- **F** On macOS, system audio sits behind the *Screen & System Audio Recording* permission; the
  2026-10-26 review already noted a tap without permission **records silence with no error**.
- **I** For a botless recorder, silent capture is the failure that loses a meeting, and it is
  discovered too late. Kitokey keeps the audio (ADR-0004), so a *partial* failure is recoverable;
  a *silent* one is not.

### The OS privacy indicator

- **F** macOS: an orange dot when the mic is in use, **a purple dot when system audio is being
  recorded**, the apps listed at the top of Control Center ([Apple, Control Center](https://support.apple.com/en-au/guide/mac-help/mchl50f94f8f) *vendor*).
- **F** GNOME Shell shows a mic indicator whenever a stream records, "so that capture doesn't happen
  unnoticed" ([GNOME Shell MR 729](https://gitlab.gnome.org/GNOME/gnome-shell/-/merge_requests/729/pipelines) *vendor*).
- **F** Apps that hold the mic open draw complaints: Zoom's dot that stayed on after calls
  ([The Register, 2022](https://www.theregister.com/2022/02/10/zoom_mac_microphone/) *secondary*;
  [Zoom community](https://community.zoom.com/t5/Meetings/Why-is-the-Zoom-app-listening-on-my-microphone-when-not-in-a/m-p/87495) *secondary*).
  A user guesses OBS keeps it lit to feed its meters (same thread, **U**).
- **I** The convention is: open the mic **only while a test panel is on screen**, close it when the
  panel hides; never a resting meter. Zoom, Meet and Teams all test inside a window the user opened.

### Kitokey today

**F** Levels are shown while recording (`modules/shell/src/levels.rs`); the 2026-10-26 review notes
a warning after 10 s of silence. No test before the start was found.

### Recommendation — **M**

*Vérifier le son*, a panel opened on demand (small window, Settings, and once at first run):

1. **Two live meters, *Moi* and *La réunion***, the mic opened only while the panel is visible
   (the OS indicator then lights for a reason the user just gave).
2. **A loopback check for *La réunion***: play a short chime on the default output and confirm the
   `remote` capture heard it — "Kitokey entend bien le son de l'ordinateur ✓". Nobody read does this.
   **Not while a call is detected**: on loudspeakers the chime would reach the call through the mic;
   then fall back to "play something, watch the meter".
3. **The same two health marks during the recording**, per side, in the small window and in the
   tray icon: *La réunion — aucun son depuis 2 min*. Extends what exists.
4. On macOS later: the silent-tap case named in plain words with a button to the right pane of
   System Settings.

Do not build: a Zoom-style record-and-replay of the voice (the meter answers the question), a test
that runs by itself at each start (lights the indicator for nothing), an automatic gain.

**Readings.**
- **I** The leaders converge on: **one live sign** (bars or a dot), **one way back** (click → the
  app), **a drag**, and **a setting to hide it**. Stop is inside the app at Granola, on the bar at
  Loom and Zoom.
- **F** What users resent is a bar that **comes back after being hidden** (Zoom), **cannot be
  turned off** (Teams), or **covers shared content** (Zoom).
- **F** **On GNOME under Wayland, an app cannot keep itself on top or place itself**: Tauri's issue
  was closed because "Wayland doesn't have an api for setting always-on-top"
  ([tauri #3117](https://github.com/tauri-apps/tauri/issues/3117)); GTK's keep-above does nothing
  there ([GNOME bug 707079](https://bugzilla.gnome.org/show_bug.cgi?id=707079)); "no programmatic
  way… by design" ([GNOME Discourse](https://discourse.gnome.org/t/any-way-to-set-window-always-on-top-programmatically/31579)) *vendor forums*.
  **I** A floating pill is a Windows and macOS shape; on GNOME the always-visible place is the top bar.

### Recommendation — **M**, by system

- **GNOME**: the **tray icon is the indicator** — a distinct recording state, the elapsed time in
  its menu, *Arrêter* first. The small window stays as is (D34/D35); the user can pin it with the
  window menu's *Always on Top*, which Mutter offers. Do not fight Wayland.
- **Windows and macOS**: a **pill**, about 200×36 px: a red dot, the timer, two thin bars
  (*Moi* / *La réunion*, the health of §1), a stop button, a click opens the small window. Draggable,
  position remembered, **off by a setting**, never shown again once hidden for the session.
- Shortcut start/stop already exists (D20): keep it the way out when everything is hidden (Loom's lesson).

Do not build: a live-transcript panel over other apps (Fireflies), a pill that reappears by itself,
a GNOME Shell extension to force always-on-top.

**Trend.** **F** The cloud leaders built on not keeping audio (Granola, Notion) or keep a lossy
copy in their cloud (MP3, AAC). **I** No one reviewed keeps lossless audio; Kitokey is alone there,
and so it is alone with the disk bill.

### What an hour weighs

Computed (**I**, arithmetic), except FLAC (the decider's measure, given in the brief).

| Form | One track, per hour | Two tracks, per hour | A year at 1 h/working day (220 h) |
|---|---|---|---|
| WAV 48 kHz, 2 ch, 24 bits (today) | 1,037 MB | **2,074 MB** | 456 GB |
| FLAC of the same | — | **≈ 1,000 MB** (measured) | ≈ 220 GB |
| WAV 16 kHz, 1 ch, 16 bits (what Whisper hears) | 115 MB | 230 MB | 51 GB |
| AAC 96 kbps (Zoom-like) | 43 MB | 86 MB | 19 GB |
| Opus 48 kbps, 1 ch | 21.6 MB | 43 MB | 9.5 GB |
| Opus 32 kbps, 1 ch | 14.4 MB | 29 MB | 6.3 GB |

**I** With "kept forever by default", today's format fills a 512 GB laptop in about a year of one
meeting a day. FLAC halves that, it does not solve it. A lossy copy at 32–48 kbps is ~23–35× smaller than FLAC.

### What the literature says about Opus and recognition

- **F** Opus at the bitrates its makers recommend for speech: **24 kb/s gives fullband**; the RFC's
  "sweet spot" for fullband speech is **28–40 kb/s** ([Xiph wiki](https://wiki.xiph.org/Opus_Recommended_Settings), [RFC 7587](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc7587) *vendor/standard*).
- **F** Khare, Sundaram, Wu (Amazon, 2020): on a non-Whisper acoustic model, **WER rises 12.6 %
  relative at 16 kbps**; it degrades sharply below that; above 32 kbps on a channel the loss is small
  ([arXiv 2002.00122](https://arxiv.org/abs/2002.00122)).
- **F** NoLACE (2023): on a Conformer ASR, Opus raises WER by about one point at 6 kb/s and converges
  towards clean speech at higher rates ([arXiv 2309.14521](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2309.14521), search only).
- **F** Kim, Hong, An (ICTC 2025), **Whisper base**, Korean noisy speech, **MP3** 32–192 kbps: CER
  "stable at 24–27 %, regardless of the bit rate" once the sample rate is ≥ 16 kHz; 16 kHz/64 kbps
  cost **+1.2 points** of CER against 44.1 kHz/192 kbps for 75 % less size
  ([ICTC 2025 paper](https://d2j16w31g89z0j.cloudfront.net/site/ictc2025/abs/P03-25.pdf), read in full).
- **F** At 4.5 kbps (far below speech settings), MP3 and Opus leave Whisper near 19–20 % WER
  ([arXiv 2603.09034](https://arxiv.org/html/2603.09034)): low bitrates do hurt.
- **U** **No study found of Whisper (or Parakeet) on Opus at 24–64 kbps, nor any in French, nor on
  meetings.** The evidence says "above ~32 kbps a codec costs little", on other models and languages.
- **F** Whisper resamples everything to 16 kHz mono before it hears it (ADR-0004 already derives that
  copy). **I** 48 kHz and 24 bits serve listening and future processing (echo cancelling, a weak
  voice), not today's engine.

### Recommendation

1. **FLAC once the recording stops — S–M.** Already ADR-0004's plan, keeps its rule "nothing lost".
   Halves the disk. FLAC plays in WebKitGTK (GStreamer) and Chromium (WebView2); **U** for WKWebView,
   check with the Mac cycle.
2. **A bench before any lossy decision — S.** The D76 trial meeting, transcribed from FLAC and from
   Opus 32 and 48 kbps (Ogg), same engine, diffed word by word. Refuted is delivered.
3. **If the bench holds, a *compact* retention step — M, and an ADR amendment.** A setting beside
   the existing "free the audio after N days": *after N days, keep a compact copy* (Opus 48 kbps per
   track, ≈ 43 MB/h) and drop the master. This contradicts ADR-0004 rule 1 and the decider's "source
   de vérité": it is the decider's call, not a default. CNPD's line (keep minutes, drop audio once
   approved, review of 2026-11-03) supports offering it.

Do not build: lossy at capture (ADR-0004's lesson: what is reduced at the microphone cannot be
recovered), a format picker in Settings, 16-bit masters to save space (D1's weak voice).

**Readings.**
- **F** Every live system above is re-done or completed after the call (Otter's second pass for
  speakers, Granola's enhancement, Meetily's enhance). **I** A live transcript is a *preview*; the

- **I** Live is a must-have for accessibility (captions; the meeting apps already give them) and
  for catching up after a distraction (Granola's users). It is a distraction as a main surface
  (Granola's own conclusion).

### Recommendation

**Do not build a live transcript this cycle.** Instead, **S**: during the recording, show *who
speaks* — the two side meters of §1 — and, if the decider wants "something is happening", a word
count or a line "La transcription commencera à l'arrêt". If live is still wanted, a **spike, M**:
Kyutai `stt-1b-en_fr` (the only open French streaming model found) beside a real call on the
decider's laptop, measuring CPU, GPU, dropped audio in the master, and latency; the batch transcript
stays the record. Refuted is delivered.

Do not build: live AI notes or answers during the call (Fireflies, Copilot), a live panel over
other apps, a live pass that replaces the batch one.

**Readings.**
- **I** What works: **one default, a meeting type that chooses the *template*, and the model as an
  after-the-fact or advanced choice**. Granola hides it behind Advanced *and* a paid plan; Superwhisper
  ties it to a named mode the user built; nobody asks for a model *before* a meeting.
- **I** Kitokey already has the right shape (*Refaire…*). What is missing is the meeting type (the
  2026-10-26 review's #8, still *Later*), not a model picker.

### Recommendation — **S**

- Keep the default in Settings; **no choice at the start of a recording**.
- In *Refaire…*, put transcription and minutes models under a closed *Avancé* disclosure, the
  last choice remembered for that meeting only.
- When meeting types come (*Later*), a type may carry a model, as a Superwhisper mode does — only
  then.

Do not build: a model picker on the start button, an "Auto" router that picks a model by itself
(an opaque cost and a quality the user cannot explain), per-meeting engine benchmarks.

## 7. Trends 2025–2026

1. **F** **Live AI moves aside, post-meeting becomes the product** at the design-led leader
   (Granola, via [Sacra, 2025-10](https://sacra.com/chat/h/bb316bee-0809-4c7d-a859-4095ed2142a1/)),
   while sales-led tools sell live assistance ([Fireflies Live Assist](https://guide.fireflies.ai/articles/2679406774-live-assist-sales-assist-fireflies-desktop-app-real-time-notes-answers-suggestions)).
2. **F** **The cloud leaders keep no audio** (Granola, Notion) — so a silent capture failure loses
   the meeting for good ([Granola](https://docs.granola.ai/help-center/taking-notes/transcription); [Anarlog](https://anarlog.so/blog/granola-ai-complaints/) *rival*).
3. **F** **Silent capture is the botless failure**, documented by vendors themselves (Granola's
   green/grey check, Wispr's mic fallback); **no one checks capture before the call**.
4. **F** **Small open streaming models arrived in 2026** (Moonshine v2, Feb; Voxtral Realtime, Feb;
   Kyutai en_fr) — **U** none measured in French meetings beside a live call.
5. **F** **The OS now shows capture**: macOS's purple dot for system audio
   ([Apple](https://support.apple.com/en-au/guide/mac-help/mchl50f94f8f)), GNOME's mic indicator; and
   regulators ask for a visible indicator throughout (AEPD, review of 2026-11-03).
6. **F** **Compact meeting windows became the norm, and so did complaints that they can't be turned
   off** (Teams [MC1266027](https://mc.merill.net/message/MC1266027/compare/2026-07-07T16-52-51-467Z/2026-08-11T17-51-28-413Z), [Q&A](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/answers/questions/5572130/how-to-disable-meeting-compact-view); Zoom's returning bar).
7. **F** **Model choice exists, behind "Advanced" and a paid plan, with "Auto" as default**
   (Granola recipes; Superwhisper modes for power users).
8. **F** **Calm, hand-made identities** over glossy AI branding (Granola's 2026 rebrand, Linear's
   refresh); GNOME's own new font (Adwaita Sans, GNOME 48).

## 9. Do not build

- A live transcript panel or live AI notes during the call (Granola's own lesson; CPU beside a call).
- Lossy encoding at capture; 16-bit masters; a format picker.
- A model picker on the start button; an "Auto" router choosing models by itself.
- A floating pill on GNOME forced on top through an extension or XWayland.
- A pill or popup that comes back after the user hid it; more than one notification per meeting.
- A test that opens the mic at each start or at rest; a resting level meter.
- Record-and-replay voice tests (Zoom's) — the meter answers the question.
- A component library, a custom font, a mascot, a theme picker.
