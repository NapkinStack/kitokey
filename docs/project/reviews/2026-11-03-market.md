## 1. Wholeheard against the field today

**Facts** (the repository, or a page above):

- **Linux, packaged, botless, local, from the system's updater**: still nobody. Meetily: from source.
  OpenWhispr: a `.deb`/AppImage, a meeting cap by plan, no Copr found. Granola, Foresight, Summit: no Linux.
- **The audio stays, and the user decides when it goes**: `docs/privacy.md` already lets a meeting's
  sound alone go to the trash, by hand or after a number of days. That is exactly the CNPD's shape
  (keep the minutes, drop the audio once they are approved), and re-listening is what it says settles
  a dispute: D75's "plays its second".
- **Plain files in the user's folder**: Granola locked its readable local cache in 2026 and users
  complained (*secondary*: [superframeworks](https://superframeworks.com/articles/granola-encrypted-local-notes),
  [Week in voice AI](https://weekinvoiceai.substack.com/p/week-in-voice-ai12-granola-scramble)).
- **French UI**: still rare (Summit: EN/DE/ES/RU; Granola: English).

**Weaknesses (facts):** Me / Others only, while OpenWhispr now ships a speaker picker; no meeting types;
Ubuntu targeted at the older LTS only; on the Mac, a free local rival from Google (Foresight, 2026-10).

**Assumptions (I):**

- Foresight, if it gains French and the call's two sides, is the macOS threat that matters before the
  Mac cycle: free and local, from Google. Wholeheard's Mac price then rests on French, the
  passage-and-second check, the call's two sides, and one tool across three systems.
- Fathom inside a suite of agents widens away from the job, as Granola and Otter did (10-30, reading 1).
- The start reminder plus the tray state is probably the "indicator for the whole session" the AEPD
  asks; whether the tray counts as "visible and active": **U**, not legal advice.
- Article 50(2): Wholeheard reaches the market after 2026-08-02, so no grace if it applies at all;
  whether it applies to minutes a user's chosen model writes: **U**, a lawyer's. The minutes already
  name the service and model that wrote them (D51), which is the cheap honest form either way.

## 2. Trends

1. **F** **Local-first is now a big company's demo** (Google's Foresight, Gemma 4) — **I** local alone
   sells less every month; finish, French and Linux are what is left to own.
2. **F** **Consolidation**: Fathom into Superhuman (Sep), Granola's lock-down of its data (2026).
   **I** Users who were burned look for tools whose files they own.
3. **F** **Regulators move from "notify at start" to "visible throughout, per session, stop by
   itself"** (AEPD) and **"delete the audio once the minutes are approved"** (CNPD). Wholeheard's
   defaults already lean that way.
4. **F** **Speaker labels reach the local tools' free tiers** (OpenWhispr's picker, Anarlog's manual
   labelling). Still the first functional complaint (10-26).
5. **F** **Linux distributions moved under the plan**: Fedora 45 today, Ubuntu 26.04 since April; PPAs
   are switched off at each release upgrade.
