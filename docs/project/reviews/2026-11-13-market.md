# Market UX scan: meeting recorders and minutes (desk research, 2026-11-13)

Desk research by an independent session for the UX audit of Kitokey 0.2.5, 2026-11-13; read for [cycle 10's framing](../cycles/10-kitokey-ready-when-you-come-back.md). The earlier reviews: [2026-10-26 (UX)](2026-10-26-ux.md) and [2026-11-06](2026-11-06-market.md). Kept as written.

Method: WebSearch and WebFetch only. Official help centres, changelogs and design docs come first. Third-party sources are labelled **[3rd]**. "acc." means accessed 2026-11-13 because the page is undated. Pages showing "updated N days ago" are treated as acc. 2026-11-13.
Sections marked **Opinion** are mine. Everything else is a sourced fact. "Not found" means I could not confirm the point in a source I could read. It does not mean the feature is absent.
Limits: help.otter.ai returned HTTP 403, and I found no official Bluedot, Krisp or Zoom help article, so those products depend on secondary sources.

## 1. Products

### Granola (desktop macOS/Windows plus mobile; no bot)
- **Start/stop:** capture starts when you click the notification, open a meeting after its start time, click "New Note", or stay in a note until the meeting starts. Nothing is transcribed unless a note is open. You can stop with the stop button or with Stop/Resume in the transcript panel. Auto-stop triggers when the call seems to have ended, after 15 minutes without new audio, or when the computer sleeps. Calendar meetings also use the scheduled end time. Ad-hoc meetings rely on the call app releasing the mic. Back-to-back calls can merge into one transcript unless you stop between them. https://docs.granola.ai/help-center/taking-notes/transcription (acc.)
- **While recording:** green animated bars at the bottom of the note. A **floating live-meeting indicator** sits on the right edge of the screen when you are in another app, and can be turned off in Settings > Preferences > General. Same source.
- **After stop:** the transcript, your typed notes and calendar details are combined into "enhanced notes". https://docs.granola.ai/help-center/getting-started/granola-101.md (acc.). No processing time or ready notification found.
- **Presentation:** the transcript is shown as chat bubbles: grey on the left for system audio ("Them"), green on the right for the mic ("Me"). Display-name speaker tags exist for Meet, Zoom and Teams. You can copy or delete chunks and search. Per-line timestamps are not mentioned. The notes have an "Enhanced" tab. https://docs.granola.ai/help-center/taking-notes/transcription and https://docs.granola.ai/help-center/taking-notes/customise-notes-with-templates.md (acc.)
- **Redo:** after the meeting, the dropdown on the **"Enhanced" tab** lets you pick a template, and doing so **regenerates** the notes in that format. During the meeting you insert a template with `/`. You can **resume transcription on an ended note and regenerate** to include the new audio. Version history is not found. Templates source as above, plus the transcription page.
- **Model choice:** none for notes. For Granola Chat, the Basic plan picks the model automatically ("Auto") and Business allows a manual choice of model. A January 2026 article routed Auto to GPT-5.1 for single meetings and Claude Sonnet for multi-meeting chats. https://docs.granola.ai/help-center/getting-more-from-your-notes/understanding-model-selection-in-granola-chat (acc.)
- **Privacy:** no bot. Granola "does not record or save audio or video at any point during the call", and audio goes to an unnamed transcription provider (transcription page). A vendor blog names Deepgram and AssemblyAI and says the audio is deleted: https://www.granola.ai/blog/granola-free-trial-get-started (acc.)
- **Onboarding:** Google/Microsoft SSO, then calendar access ("Select all"), then the macOS mic and system-audio prompts (granted automatically on Windows), then a **2-minute demo video from the co-founder** that plays once, plus a **demo note** on the home view. Templates offered at first are based on the "user type" picked at install. https://docs.granola.ai/help-center/getting-started/setting-up-granola-for-the-first-time (acc.), templates page.
- **Dates shown:** the calendar supplies attendees, time, date and title (setup page). No timestamp on the generated notes was found.

### Otter.ai (bot "Notetaker" plus desktop app since 2025-10-07)
- **Start:** the bot auto-joins Zoom, Teams and Meet from a connected calendar, or you send it to a meeting. https://otter.ai/ (acc.). The desktop app for Mac and Windows records **without a bot**: https://otter.ai/blog/introducing-the-otter-desktop-app (2025-10-07).
- **While recording:** live transcription with words shown "as they're spoken" and speaker recognition (otter.ai, acc.). A floating widget was not found.
- **After stop:** "instant summaries" is marketing copy with no timing given (otter.ai). **[3rd]** One review says the desktop app only records, and transcript and summary are opened in the web app: https://wf.lindy.ai/blog/otter-review (2026).
- **Redo:** **[3rd]** users can regenerate a summary after correcting the transcript or speakers, and regenerating **overwrites manual edits** to the outline. https://research.contrary.com/company/otter (acc.). Not confirmed by Otter itself (help centre returned 403).
- **Model choice, onboarding:** not found.

### Fireflies.ai (bot "Notetaker" or desktop "Take Notes" without a bot)
- **Start:** a **Take Notes** button at the top right of the desktop app. Auto-detection covers conferencing apps, FaceTime, Discord, browsers and phone calls, and shows a desktop notification with **Take Notes** (no bot) or **Invite Notetaker** (bot). There is a customisable **hotkey** and an "Automatically take notes" setting. https://guide.fireflies.ai/articles/6666374717-how-to-record-meetings-without-a-bot-on-the-fireflies-desktop-app and https://guide.fireflies.ai/articles/1208704416-getting-started-with-the-fireflies-desktop-app (both acc.)
- **While recording:** the **Floating Pane** opens on its own. It shows the live transcript and notes plus AskFred, can be dragged, resized, collapsed to a compact toolbar or made full screen, and is **hidden during screen sharing** by default. It has **Pause/Resume**. **Stop** asks **Save Recording / Delete**. Getting-started page.
- **After stop:** a **Quick Summary within seconds**, then the meeting opens in the post-meeting workspace. **About 1 minute later** the full AI summary replaces it. No post-stop notification is described. Same page.
- **Presentation:** the summary sits in the Notes tab in the left panel. The default template contains Keywords, Overview, Notes, **Time-stamped Notes** (chapters linking to the transcript) and Action Items tied to speakers. Without the bot, speakers appear as "Speaker 1/2". https://guide.fireflies.ai/articles/9547055509-learn-about-the-fireflies-ai-meeting-summaries (acc.)
- **Redo:** **Customize Notes > Template** regenerates "in a few seconds", and you choose **this meeting only or all future meetings**. Switching template **costs no AI credits**. The template editor offers **Save** (future meetings) or **Save & Apply** (this meeting and future ones). You can tick or untick individual **sections**. **⋯ > Regenerate Summary** is offered "after you edit the transcript, correct speaker labels, or change the template", the result arrives in "a few minutes", and **only the owner or host** can run it. Versions are not mentioned. Same page.
- **Privacy:** the no-bot mode "does not save audio or video files". A **consent modal** appears on first Take Notes (auto-play consent message, notice in meeting chat). No-bot page.
- **Onboarding:** no wizard is documented. Settings > Permissions shows the microphone status. No mic test was found. Getting-started page.

### tl;dv (bot plus a desktop app without a bot)
- **Start:** a tray or menu-bar icon opens the app to start recording. The app **detects meetings and calls** (scheduled meetings, audio calls, Slack huddles, browser meetings) and prompts you. Toggle in Settings > Desktop App > Live meeting notifications. Bot auto-recording is recommended for Meet, Zoom and Teams. https://intercom.help/tldv/en/articles/14433922-desktop-app (acc.). The desktop app captures mic and system audio, not video: https://intercom.help/tldv/en/articles/14433337-bot-free-recording (acc.)
- **Onboarding/permissions:** macOS asks for mic and system audio. If the user clicks "Don't Allow", the article walks through **Start recording > Enable microphone / Enable system audio**, then toggling in System Settings and **restarting the app**. Desktop-app article.
- **Presentation:** AI notes appear **to the left of the video**, grouped by topic with a "gist" and **timestamped notes that link to the moment**. https://intercom.help/tldv/en/articles/7198123-ai-meeting-notes (2025-11-24)
- **Redo:** "Change the template easily" (same article). Templates can apply per meeting or automatically by rules. Minutes are **emailed to all participants** when the call ends. https://tldv.io/features/ai-meeting-minutes/ (modified 2025-05-15). An explicit regenerate control was not found.
- **Model choice:** not found. The marketing page says tl;dv "trains its AI on real-world meeting data".

### Fathom (bot plus a Mac desktop mode without a bot)
- **Start:** the bot auto-joins "as soon as you join or start the meeting" according to the team's recording rules. A Mac desktop capture without a bot exists (full video, audio plus transcript, or transcript only); Windows "on the way". https://www.fathom.ai/product (acc.)
- **While recording:** "live summaries update during the call". After stop: "the moment your meeting ends, a full recap arrives". Same page. **[3rd]** Reported delays vary from seconds to about 2 minutes: https://zapier.com/blog/fathom-features (acc.)
- **Redo:** on the call page, pick a summary type, then the **gear icon**, add instructions, and **Regenerate Summary**. Optionally **Apply to Future Summaries**. A pencil icon refines the instructions and an arrow **reverts to the default**. Desktop users without the bot can only customise in the web app. https://help.fathom.video/en/articles/3239809 (acc.). Versions are not mentioned.
- **Presentation:** Ask Fathom citations link to the exact moment in the transcript (product page).
- **Model choice:** not named. Privacy: SOC 2 Type II, HIPAA (product page).

### Notion AI Meeting Notes (desktop captures mic plus system audio; browser and mobile capture mic only)
- **Start:** `/meet` on any page, the "Meeting notes" sidebar, or a **notification when the desktop app detects you joining a video call**. Notion Calendar offers "Join and transcribe". You start with **Start transcribing** once consent is obtained. https://www.notion.com/help/ai-meeting-notes (acc.)
- **Stop:** **Stop**, or **automatic detection of the call ending** from meeting-app presence and audio activity, without using audio or video content. You can resume via the slider icon, then **Resume transcription**. Same page.
- **After stop:** the **summary generates automatically**. Minimum **1 minute** of audio or 300 transcribed characters. No timing given.
- **Presentation:** a new line at each speaker change. Speaker labels are English only, mainly for 1:1 calls. **Summary citations link to transcript lines** (hover previews, click jumps).
- **Redo:** an **Instructions** dropdown at the bottom of the block (default "Auto"). **Retry summary** regenerates with new instructions. **+ Add custom instructions** opens a template page. You can **Set as default**. Suggested "skills" appear during and after the meeting.
- **Model choice:** none exposed. Subprocessors listed: OpenAI, Anthropic, Baseten, xAI, AssemblyAI.
- **Privacy:** subprocessors do not store audio. A temporary local copy is deleted after processing or within 24 h. Optional **local audio storage keeps the last 10 recordings** (off by default).
- **Onboarding:** screen-recording and system-audio permissions. A **consent reminder** offers a text message, a voice message played through the speakers, or verbal consent, and the default is chosen at first use. Cap of 10 h/day. Offline not supported.

### Krisp (on-device audio layer; bot optional)
- **Start:** pick the Krisp Speaker and Krisp Microphone in the call app, then turn on **Note Taker**. A calendar connection gives "one-click meeting access". https://krisp.ai/ai-meeting-assistant/ (acc.). The AI Note Taker page adds **auto-open**, note generation and **app exclusions**. Recording notifications cover Teams as well as Zoom. A "Device Setup Reminder" can be turned off. https://krisp.launchnotes.io/announcements/ann_OuDHrBhrAXTsg (2025-11-04)
- **Privacy:** English transcription runs **on-device**, with "no voice data visible to Krisp servers" (product page).
- **Redo:** **user edits are preserved when the Summary is regenerated**. **Visual indicators distinguish AI text from user edits**. A **Summarize button on the transcript page lets you generate the summary after assigning speakers**. "The Gist" gives a short wrap-up with chapter navigation. https://krisp.launchnotes.io/announcements/ann_AsLz8YC5Yg2sq (2023-10-26). **[3rd]** Template editor: https://meetgeek.ai/blog/meetgeek-vs-krisp (Sept 2026).
- **Model choice, onboarding details:** not found.

### MacWhisper (local Whisper/Parakeet, Mac)
- **Start:** **Meeting Detected** notification with a **Record** button, dismissed after 10 s. Covers Zoom, Teams, Webex, Chrome, Slack and Discord (not FaceTime). There is also a **Meeting Ended** notification that can stop the recording. Manual start from the menu. The feature is beta, "may lose data", and is absent from the Mac App Store build. Needs Screen Recording and Microphone permissions. https://docs.macwhisper.com/article/30-record-meetings (updated 2025-07-15)
- **After stop:** the recording appears in the history sidebar, and you "wait for transcription". All other participants are grouped as one speaker. Same page.
- **Model choice (exposed):** local Whisper and **Parakeet** models (Parakeet added in v13, 2025-06-27, Pro): https://9to5mac.com/2025/06/27/macwhisper-13-supports-nvidia-parakeet-transcription-model/ . v14 (2026-07-14) adds an editor view, faster speaker reassignment, cloud Deepgram/Gladia with your own key, and a "redesigned AI services screen": https://9to5mac.com/2026/07/14/macwhisper-14-launches-with-a-new-transcript-editor-faster-performance-more/ . **[3rd]** Live meeting transcription (beta) needs Parakeet or WhisperKit: https://releasebot.io/updates/macwhisper (acc.)

## 2. UI guidance relevant to a recording bar, list + detail, settings, long local jobs, errors

### GNOME HIG / libadwaita
- **Progress bars:** use them for tasks over about 30 s. The label states completed work or remaining time ("about…"). Use one combined bar across stages and mention stages only when they matter. Add pause or cancel for heavy work. Place the bar **inline near the content** and avoid progress windows. A **thin bar attached to the bottom of the header bar** suits background work. https://developer.gnome.org/hig/patterns/feedback/progress-bars.html (acc.)
- **Spinners:** show one for operations over about 3 s. **Delay its appearance** when duration varies. Show one at a time, next to the element it concerns. For long runs, add a label with percentage, time or sub-steps. Switch to a bar beyond 30 s. https://developer.gnome.org/hig/patterns/feedback/spinners.html (acc.)
- **Toasts:** for individual events only, never ongoing states. Short title. A button only if it is directly useful (Undo). Bottom-centred. https://developer.gnome.org/hig/patterns/feedback/toasts.html (acc.)
- **Banners:** for **persistent states** such as offline or read-only. They do not auto-hide, have one title and an optional button, and should not be used for events. https://developer.gnome.org/hig/patterns/feedback/banners.html (acc.)
- **Notifications:** for events while the user is in other apps, e.g. "a download or other long-running operation has finished". Use toasts when relevance is in-app only. Up to 3 actions. **Withdraw stale notifications**. Don't rely solely on notifications: the window must show the same state. https://developer.gnome.org/hig/patterns/feedback/notifications.html (acc.)
- **Placeholder pages (empty states):** use the illustration style only for the app's initial unavoidable empty view and the symbolic style elsewhere. Give a title stating the state, an optional line on how to add content, and a key action, where a suggested-style button "can be appropriate". https://developer.gnome.org/hig/patterns/feedback/placeholders.html (acc.)
- **Adaptive:** desktop minimum 1024×600, phone 360×294. Use breakpoints to switch layouts. Lists scale well. Content needs a max width. https://developer.gnome.org/hig/guidelines/adaptive.html (acc.). A utility pane must overlap the content when narrow, with F9 to toggle: https://developer.gnome.org/hig/patterns/containers/utility-panes.html (acc.)
- **libadwaita 1.5:** AdwDialog shows as a **bottom sheet when the window is ≤ 450 px wide or ≤ 360 px high**, otherwise floating. AdwPreferencesDialog and AdwAlertDialog replace the old windows. https://gnome.pages.gitlab.gnome.org/libadwaita/doc/1.5/migrating-to-adaptive-dialogs.html
- **libadwaita 1.6 (2024-09-13):** **AdwSpinner** replaces GtkSpinner; it keeps animating with animations off, caps at 64 px, and has AdwSpinnerPaintable for status pages. Public **AdwBottomSheet** gains a bottom-bar mode. **System accent colours** (9 presets via the portal; apps must use accent variables and not assume blue). **AdwButtonRow** for list rows that act as buttons. Restyled AdwAlertDialog. AdwMultiLayoutView. Default minimum window size 360×200. https://blogs.gnome.org/alicem/2024/09/
- **libadwaita 1.7:** AdwToggleGroup, AdwInlineViewSwitcher, AdwWrapBox. **1.8 (GNOME 49, Sept 2025):** AdwShortcutsDialog and AdwShortcutLabel. **[3rd]** https://linuxiac.com/libadwaita-1-8-arrives-alongside-gnome-49/ (2025-09). Doc index: https://gnome.pages.gitlab.gnome.org/libadwaita/doc/1.8/

### Apple HIG
- **Progress indicators:** prefer determinate. Advance at an even pace (90% in 5 s then 10% in 5 min "can feel deceptive"). **Keep indicators moving**, and if a process stalls, explain why and what to do. Switch from indeterminate to determinate when possible, but don't swap a spinner for a bar. Avoid vague labels like "loading". Keep a consistent location. Offer Cancel, and Pause if cancelling loses work, with an alert when cancelling discards progress. macOS: a spinner for background work, usually unlabelled. https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/progress-indicators (changelog 2023-09-12)
- **Liquid Glass (added 2025-06-09, updated 2025-09-09):** use it only for the functional layer of controls and navigation. "**Don't use Liquid Glass in the content layer**." Use it **sparingly** on custom controls. Use the regular variant for text-heavy surfaces. Reduce Transparency and Increase Contrast settings change its look. https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/materials

### Material 3 Expressive (Google, May 2025)
- Research: 46 studies, over 18,000 participants. Key elements were found **up to 4× faster**. Users 45+ performed on par with younger users. Breaking familiar patterns (a list turned into an album-art grid, actions without labels) **lowered usability**, and a "strong minority" preferred calmer designs. Tactics: colour, shape, size, motion, containment. https://design.google/library/expressive-material-design-google-research (I/O 2025)
- New **loading indicator** (shape morph) for waits **under 5 s**. **Wavy linear and circular progress** for longer processes. https://9to5google.com/2025/05/16/material-3-expressive-loading-indicator/ (2025-05-16)

### Accessibility (WCAG 2.2, W3C)
- New AA criteria: **2.4.11 Focus Not Obscured**, **2.5.8 Target Size ≥ 24×24 px** (or spacing), **2.5.7 Dragging Movements** (requires a single-pointer alternative). A-level: **3.2.6 Consistent Help**, **3.3.7 Redundant Entry**. AA: **3.3.8 Accessible Authentication**. https://www.w3.org/WAI/standards-guidelines/wcag/new-in-22/ (updated 2023-10-05)

## 3. Opinion (not sourced; my reading of the above)
- The leading pattern for redo is a **template switcher on the summary itself** that regenerates in place (Granola Enhanced tab, Fireflies Customize Notes, Zoom dropdown, Jamie after-the-fact). The best versions add a **scope choice** (this meeting or future ones, as Fireflies and Fathom do), a **revert** (Zoom, Fathom's "revert to default"), and **protection of user edits** (Krisp). Overwriting edits silently, as Otter does per a third-party source, is the anti-pattern.
- For local Whisper plus LLM, the cloud leaders' "seconds" expectations (Fireflies' Quick Summary and full summary about 1 minute later, Fathom's "moment it ends") do not hold. The GNOME and Apple guidance points to a **determinate, labelled, inline progress bar with stages folded into one estimate, plus cancel**, then a **desktop notification only if the window is unfocused**, withdrawn when opened.
- No leader documents a summary "generated at" time or version history. That is an opening, not a convention, so keep it modest: a timestamp plus the model or template used.
- Recording-state convention: a small **always-visible indicator outside the main window** (Granola's floating indicator, Fireflies' collapsible pane, Jamie's menu-bar stop, Apple's on-screen indicator). The usual minimum is pause/resume plus stop, with auto-stop on silence or call end as an extra.
